# Team brief — ARES Health AI

## Recruitment pitch (English)

ARES Health AI is a remote, Spanish-speaking team creating an explainable, offline-capable astronaut health trend-monitoring prototype for NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026. We welcome collaborators in Python, software engineering, health science, data analysis, product design, and technical communication. We value reliable contributions, documentation, and evidence-led problem solving. Experience levels are welcome; roles are assigned collaboratively.

## Ways to contribute

- Scientific research: verify risks, sources, and caveats.
- Data engineering: synthetic scenario schema, quality checks, traceability.
- App engineering: local dashboard and working data flow.
- UX/review: clarity, accessibility, demonstration story.
- QA: reproducible tests and final verification.

## Collaboration norms

- Primary working language: Spanish; final external artifacts: English.
- No real patients' or astronauts' health data without explicit legitimate access and safeguards.
- Each module should have a small issue / acceptance criteria / reviewer.
- Mark synthetic and experimental components clearly.
- Credit genuine contributions; do not invent affiliations.
- Confirm who may publish project assets, including datasets and logos.
